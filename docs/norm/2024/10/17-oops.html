That warning has been removed from SaxonJS 2.7, so this issue won’t surface again next October. Apologies for not getting a maintenance release out sooner. We really should have. My expectation is that we’ll ship some sort of beta test version of SaxonJS 3.0 “real soon now” and Saxon 12.6 to fix the SEF bug in 12.5 shortly before or after that.
